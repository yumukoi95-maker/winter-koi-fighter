PS5 DUALSENSE CONTROLLER

Connect via USB or Bluetooth and press any button. Supports 2 Controllers or 1 Controller + 1 Keyboard!

  • Left Stick / D-Pad: Move, Crouch & Jump
  • ▢ Square: Light Punch (Fast combo starter)
  • △ Triangle: Heavy Kick (High knockback)
  • ✕ Cross: Special / Sky Launcher
  • ◯ Circle / L1: Energy Guard / Block

2-PLAYER KEYBOARD LAYOUT
PLAYER 1 (WASD + F / G / H / R):

• Move / Jump / Crouch: W, A, S, D

• Light Punch: F • Heavy Kick: G

• Special / Launcher: H • Rage Art: R

• Block: E / Left Shift (or hold Back)

PLAYER 2 (Arrows + J / K / L / U):

• Move / Jump / Crouch: Arrow Keys

• Light Punch: J • Heavy Kick: K

• Special / Launcher: L • Rage Art: U

• Block: I / Right Shift (or hold Back)

❄️ HALLO WINTER COMBO TIPS:
• Chain Light Punch ➔ Light Punch ➔ Special/Launcher to launch opponents into the air and juggle them!
• When your HP drops below 35%, your RAGE AURA ignites—press R1/R2 (or R / U) to unleash your fighter's cinematic Rage Art!
